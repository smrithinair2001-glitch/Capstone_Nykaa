# Size-Exchange Policy

Apparel and Footwear orders are eligible for a free one-time size exchange within the same 30-day return window that applies to returns in those categories, provided the replacement size is in stock at the time of request. If the requested size is unavailable, the customer is offered a refund instead of an exchange rather than being left waiting on backorder. A size exchange is logged and fulfilled as a reverse pickup of the original item paired with a fresh forward shipment of the new size, rather than as a swap at the doorstep, so both legs follow the normal reverse-pickup and delivery SLAs described elsewhere. Beauty, Electronics, and Home items are not eligible for size exchange since they are not sized the way apparel and footwear are; a customer asking to "exchange" one of these should instead be routed to the standard return-and-reorder flow.
