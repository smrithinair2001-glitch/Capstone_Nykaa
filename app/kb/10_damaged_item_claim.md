# Damaged-Item Claim Process

A customer reporting an item damaged on arrival should be asked for photos of the product and its packaging, which are used to determine whether the damage happened in transit or before dispatch; no physical inspection visit is required for this first step. Once photos are reviewed and the claim is approved, Nykaa ships a replacement immediately without waiting for the damaged item to be picked up first, which is the key difference from a standard return flow. If no replacement stock exists, a full refund is issued within 3 business days instead, covering both the product price and any shipping fee charged. Claims must be raised within 48 hours of the delivery timestamp; claims raised later are redirected to the ordinary category return policy, since transit damage becomes hard to distinguish from damage in use after that window. Repeated damaged-item claims on the same account are flagged for manual fraud review before further replacements are auto-approved.
