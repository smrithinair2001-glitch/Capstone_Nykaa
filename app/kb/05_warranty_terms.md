# Warranty Terms by Category

Electronics sold on Nykaa carry the original manufacturer's warranty, typically 1 year for small appliances and personal gadgets, and the brand's authorized service center handles claims rather than Nykaa directly. Home category appliances carry a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects, while non-electrical Home goods such as decor or textiles carry no warranty beyond the standard return window. Beauty and Apparel items carry no warranty since they are consumable or wear items, but a genuine manufacturing defect (such as a broken zipper on arrival) is still handled as a damaged-item claim rather than a warranty claim. Footwear carries a 90-day warranty specifically against sole separation and stitching defects under normal use, excluding damage from water exposure or abrasive surfaces. In every category, the warranty is void if the product was used in a manner inconsistent with its labeled instructions.
