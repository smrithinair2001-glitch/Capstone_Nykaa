# Delivery SLAs

Standard delivery SLAs at Nykaa are 3 to 5 business days for Apparel, Footwear, and Beauty orders shipped within the same metro region, and 5 to 8 business days for orders shipped across regions. Electronics and Home category orders, which more often ship directly from a brand warehouse rather than a Nykaa fulfillment center, carry a longer SLA of 6 to 10 business days. An order is considered delayed once it crosses its promised delivery date without a "Delivered" status update, and the support agent should treat `delayed_shipment` on the order record as the source of truth rather than re-deriving it from the status field alone. Customers in serviceable pin codes outside the top 50 cities should expect an additional 2 business days on top of the ranges above due to last-mile logistics partner handoffs.
