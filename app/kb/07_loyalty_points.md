# Loyalty-Points Redemption Policy

Nykaa Rewards points are earned at 1 point per INR 100 spent on any category and become redeemable 48 hours after an order reaches "Delivered" status, which gives the return window a chance to begin before points are locked in. Points can be redeemed in multiples of 100, with 100 points worth INR 50 off a future order, and a single order may use points for at most 20% of its total value. Points earned on an order are reversed if that order is later returned or refunded, and if the customer already spent those points elsewhere, their points balance is allowed to go temporarily negative rather than blocking the return. Unredeemed points expire 12 months after they are earned, and the customer is notified by email 15 days before expiry. Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred between accounts.
