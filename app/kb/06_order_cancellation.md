# Order Cancellation Policy

An order can be cancelled free of charge any time before its status changes from "Placed" to "Shipped." Once an order has moved to "Shipped," it can no longer be cancelled in the system and the customer must instead wait for delivery and then initiate a return. For prepaid orders cancelled while still "Placed," the refund is issued to the original payment method within 2 to 4 business days; for COD orders there is nothing to refund, so cancellation simply voids the order. Bulk or custom orders (for example personalized gifting sets) cannot be cancelled once production has started, which is flagged to the customer at checkout. Repeated cancellations from the same account in a short window (more than 5 in 30 days) route the customer to a manual review queue to check for abuse before further orders are auto-confirmed.
