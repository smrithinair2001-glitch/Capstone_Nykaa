# International Shipping Restrictions

Nykaa currently ships internationally only to a limited set of countries (UAE, USA, UK, Singapore, and Australia) and only for Beauty and Apparel categories; Electronics and Home items cannot be shipped internationally because of varying plug standards, battery air-freight rules, and import certification requirements. International orders are prepaid only, since cash-on-delivery is not supported outside India, and customers are responsible for any customs duties levied by the destination country, which are not included in the Nykaa checkout price. International delivery SLAs run 10 to 18 business days depending on customs clearance, which is substantially longer than domestic SLAs, and the standard domestic return window does not apply; international returns are evaluated case by case because reverse shipping across borders is costly and slow.
