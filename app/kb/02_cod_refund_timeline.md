# Cash-on-Delivery (COD) Refund Timelines

Because a COD order has no card or wallet to refund to automatically, Nykaa first collects the customer's preferred bank account or UPI ID once a return is picked up and quality-checked. Refunds for COD orders are credited within 7 to 10 business days of the returned item passing the warehouse quality check, which is slower than the 2 to 4 business days typical for prepaid orders. If the quality check fails (for example the returned item shows signs of use beyond normal trial), the refund is held and the customer is notified within 48 hours with the reason and a chance to appeal. During sale periods such as big billion-style events, COD refund timelines may extend by an additional 3 business days due to warehouse volume, and this extension is communicated proactively via SMS and email.
