# Payment-Failure and Retry Policy

If a card, UPI, or netbanking payment fails during checkout, the customer is shown the specific failure reason when the payment gateway provides one (such as insufficient funds or bank timeout) and is offered up to 3 retries with the same method before being prompted to switch payment methods. When a payment is deducted from the customer's bank account but the order is not confirmed on Nykaa's side (a "payment success, order failure" mismatch), the amount is automatically reversed by the bank within 5 to 7 business days, and Nykaa does not need to separately process a refund since the order was never created. Customers who see repeated failures on the same card are advised to contact their bank directly, since this is most often a bank-side decline rather than a Nykaa checkout issue. Wallet and gift-card balances, once deducted for an order, are instantly restored if the order placement itself fails.
